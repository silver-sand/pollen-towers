HOW IT WORKS

      
From the LessWrong discovery

      
When researchers at LessWrong asked Fable 5.1 to hide a secret in an image, 33 of 50 images used the same family of steganographic techniques.


      

        

          
1

          
Height Encoding

          
Each letter maps to a building height. A = 6px, B = 12px, Z = 156px, 0 = 186px. Spaces become gaps wider than normal building spacing.

        

        

          
2

          
Render & Crop

          
The SVG is rendered to a bitmap (PNG or JPEG). Decoys — windows, clouds, sun, birds — add realism but carry no signal. The message survives lossy compression and resizing.

        

        

          
3

          
Blind Detection

          
The Gauge analyzes raw pixels only. It finds the ground line, measures building column heights, fits a lattice, and converts heights back to text. No AI needed.

        

        

          
4

          
Score & Verify

          
A three-gate score (snap rate × tightness × valid rate) separates real signals from coincidental skylines. Controls consistently score 0 — no false positives.
